SITE PRACTICES
10 · SAFETY & QUALITY POLICY

SAFETY & QUALITY ON SITE

At M/S Nazir Ahmad Mir, quality construction and worker safety are fundamental on every site. We inspect incoming materials, enforce basic site safety rules, and ensure work meets departmental specifications.

▶
Material Inspection: Cement (OPC/PPC) and steel (Fe-500D) are procured from approved reputable brands and checked before site entry.
▶
Aggregate Checks: Coarse aggregates and sand undergo regular silt and cleanliness checks before being used in concrete mixes.
▶
Pipeline Verification: HDPE (PE-100) and GI pipes are inspected for wall thickness and quality before deployment in trenches.
▶
Stage-Wise Supervision: Site supervisors inspect formwork, rebar placement, and joints before concrete pouring or trench backfilling.
▶
Worker Safety & Social Security: Mandatory PPE on active sites, backed by full statutory ESIC health coverage (Code: 19000337770000999) and EPFO provident fund protection (Code: JKSRN4103698000).
▶
Safe Material Staging: Secure material storage at our Budgam yard to ensure uninterrupted supply during weather disruptions.
