## [1.0.0-v5] - 2026-09-22

### Added
- **Master Loop V5 Engine**: Complete architectural upgrade with schema validation, asset registration, and document control.
- **Document Control System**: Added `docs/document-control.md` tracking document version, prepared for, revision date, and approval status.
- **Data Schemas & Validation**: Introduced `company-data.schema.json` and `asset-register.schema.json` with strict draft-07 JSON Schema validation.
- **Asset Register**: Created `data/asset-register.json` tracking source, licensing status, rights clearance, and page usage for all photographic and AI visual assets.
- **22-Page Architectural Profile**: Expanded layout to 22 pages, adding P05 Organization Chart, P09 Water Infrastructure, P13 ESG & Sustainability, and P21 Document Control.
- **Dual PDF Renderer**: Updated `generate-pdf.js` with CLI options `--mode=print` and `--mode=web`.
- **Scored QA & Fact-Check Gate**: Implemented rubric scoring (0-100 pts) per page in `docs/qa-report.md` and enforced zero `[VERIFY WITH CLIENT]` unverified markers in production exports.
